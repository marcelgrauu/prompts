Watercolor

Use the attached photo as the subject. Keep the face, hair, body, pose and outfit exactly as they are, only the style changes.

STYLE: a loose, expressive watercolour portrait painted by hand on paper, unfinished in places, like a page from an artist's sketchbook.

COLOUR: soft transparent washes taken from the photo: warm peach, rose and golden ochre on the skin, cool blue, violet and grey in the shadows and background. Alternative: the whole piece in one single colour, such as [TEAL]. The brightest areas are just the white paper left unpainted.

TEXTURE AND LIGHT: cold press paper with visible tooth, pigment pooling at the edges of each wash, soft blooms and backruns where wet paint meets wet paint, granulation in the darks, a few drips running down, small splatters around the edges, and a faint pencil underdrawing still visible under the paint. Warm sunlight on one side of the face glowing in golden ochre.

DETAIL: eyes, nose and lips painted with more care and sharper edges, hair made of quick dark wet strokes, clothes and shoulders dissolving into loose washes that fade into the white paper.

COMPOSITION: head and shoulders portrait, face slightly off centre, plenty of white paper around, the painting fading out towards the edges with an irregular, unfinished border.

AVOID: smooth digital gradients, thick opaque paint, hard black outlines, a photo look, signatures, watermarks, any text.

FORMAT: vertical 2:3, high resolution.

Charcoal

Use the attached photo as the subject. Keep the face, hair, body, pose and outfit exactly as they are, only the style changes.

STYLE: an expressive charcoal portrait drawing, fast and gestural, like a fine art study finished in one session.

COLOUR: only black, greys and the warm off white of the paper. No colour at all.

TEXTURE AND LIGHT: rough toothy drawing paper, deep velvety blacks of compressed charcoal in the shadows, smudged mid tones rubbed with the fingers, highlights lifted out with an eraser, scratchy loose hatching and energetic scribbles around the hair and the edges, charcoal dust and a few fingerprints on the paper. One strong side light, like a candle or a single lamp, so half the face falls into darkness.

DETAIL: eyes, nose and mouth drawn precisely with the most contrast, hair as wild flowing lines that drift off the head, clothes only suggested with a few quick strokes.

COMPOSITION: close crop on the face and shoulders, the face filling most of the frame, the edges of the drawing dissolving into loose strokes and bare paper.

AVOID: clean digital lines, a thin pencil look, smooth airbrushed shading, colour, signatures, any text.

FORMAT: vertical 2:3, high resolution.

Ink Wash

Use the attached photo as the subject. Keep the face, hair, body, pose and outfit exactly as they are, only the style changes.

STYLE: a traditional East Asian ink wash painting (sumi style), brush and black ink on rice paper, poetic and calm.

COLOUR: black ink diluted into many soft greys on warm ivory rice paper, optionally a very faint pale green wash in the background. The only real colour is one small red square seal stamp.

TEXTURE: confident single brush strokes, dry brush streaks where the brush runs out of ink, soft wet bleeds where the ink spreads into the paper fibres, the darkest ink kept for a few accents, visible paper fibres everywhere.

SUBJECT: the person painted with very few strokes: hair and clothes as bold flowing black strokes, the face with fine thin lines and a pale wash, the edges of the clothes dissolving into the wash.

SETTING: [a waterfall falling between misty rocks], with pine trees, bamboo stalks and leaves, a leaning willow over still water with a soft reflection, mountain peaks fading into the mist.

TYPE: a short vertical column of loose calligraphy brush marks (abstract, not readable words) and the small red square seal near a lower corner.

COMPOSITION: subject small to medium, placed in the lower third or to one side, lots of empty paper acting as mist and sky, asymmetric balance, the landscape framing the figure.

AVOID: outlines on everything, full colour, realistic perspective, readable text, signatures, watermarks.

FORMAT: vertical 2:3, high resolution.

Comic Book

Use the attached photo as the subject. Keep the face, hair, body, pose and outfit exactly as they are, only the style changes.

STYLE: a page from a modern American comic book, inked and coloured by hand, with a dramatic panel layout.

COLOUR: flat colours with a limited palette per panel: teal and deep blue for night scenes, warm orange, red and pink for action, all on slightly yellowed paper. Alternative: a black and white ink version with huge areas of solid black.

TEXTURE: bold black ink outlines with varying line weight, heavy spotted blacks in the shadows, cross hatching, printed halftone dots in the skin and backgrounds, slight colour misregistration like old printing, aged newsprint paper.

TYPE: hand lettered comic text only. One rectangular yellow caption box at the top: [MEANWHILE...]. One white speech balloon: [YOUR LINE]. One big sound effect in bold outlined letters on a jagged burst shape: [POW!]. Nothing else.

LAYOUT: the subject in one big hero panel taking most of the page, plus two or three smaller tilted panels with close ups (the eyes, a hand, a detail of the outfit), white gutters between the panels.

COMPOSITION: dynamic low camera angle, motion lines around the subject, strong contrast between light and shadow.

AVOID: superhero costumes or emblems, known characters, manga style, 3D rendering, publisher logos, extra text.

FORMAT: vertical 2:3, high resolution.

Manga

Use the attached photo as the subject. Keep the face, hair, body, pose and outfit exactly as they are, only the style changes.

STYLE: a black and white manga page with detailed ink work, like a page from a printed weekly manga volume.

COLOUR: pure black ink on white paper. Greys made only with dot screentone and hatching. No colour at all.

TEXTURE: crisp dip pen linework, fine feathered hatching in the hair, dot screentone in the shadows and on the skin, solid black hair with sharp white shine highlights, rough scratchy ink in the dark areas, a slight paper grain like a scanned page.

DETAIL: large expressive eyes with detailed irises and lashes, but the face keeps the person's real features, sharp lines on the jaw and nose, an intense gaze.

GRAPHICS: speed lines or focus lines radiating from behind the subject, a few ink splatters.

LAYOUT: either one full page splash, or a page split into three or four panels with diagonal borders: an extreme close up of the eyes, a medium shot, and the full figure.

TYPE: optional, one white speech balloon with a hand lettered line: [ARE YOU READY?]. Otherwise no text.

COMPOSITION: dramatic angles, the close up of the face dominating, clean white gutters.

AVOID: colour, cute chibi proportions, western comic style, 3D look, known characters, titles, logos.

FORMAT: vertical 2:3, high resolution.

Oil Paint

Use the attached photo as the subject. Keep the face, hair, body, pose and outfit exactly as they are, only the style changes.

STYLE: a contemporary oil portrait with heavy impasto, painted with brushes and a palette knife, so close that the paint itself becomes the subject.

COLOUR: rich, warm and saturated. Skin built from ochre, peach, rose and touches of orange and red. Shadows in deep violet, ultramarine and teal. Hair in dark umber with strokes of blue and purple. Background in [warm ochre] or neutral grey.

TEXTURE AND LIGHT: very thick paint ridges that cast tiny shadows, palette knife slabs of colour placed side by side like a mosaic, visible brush hairs and dragged strokes, wet into wet blending in the skin, canvas weave showing in the thin areas. Warm directional light from one side, glossy highlights on the ridges of paint.

DETAIL: the eyes painted with the most precision and a bright catchlight; the rest gets looser, and towards the edges the strokes turn wild and abstract, with some bare canvas and pencil lines showing.

COMPOSITION: tight crop, the face filling most of the frame, slightly off centre, the paint running to the edges.

AVOID: smooth digital painting, a photo filter look, flat colour, signatures, any text.

FORMAT: vertical 2:3, high resolution.

Pointillism

Use the attached photo as the subject. Keep the face, hair, body, pose and outfit exactly as they are, only the style changes.

STYLE: a neo impressionist pointillism painting made entirely of small dabs of pure colour that blend in the eye from a distance.

COLOUR: pure unmixed pigments placed side by side: cobalt blue, ultramarine, violet, orange, yellow, vermilion, emerald and lime. Complementary colours next to each other so everything vibrates. Glowing [golden hour] light: orange, yellow and white dots near the light, deep blue and violet in the shadows.

TEXTURE: every area built from round or slightly oval dots of thick paint, all of a similar size and slightly raised, tiny gaps showing the canvas, the dots following the direction of the forms. No lines and no blended areas.

SETTING: the subject in a luminous landscape: [a rocky coast at sunset with the sun reflecting on the sea], with trees, flowers and reflections in the water. At night: a waterfront promenade with glowing street lamps.

LIGHT: one strong light source in the scene (the sun or lamps) creating a halo of yellow and white dots and a shimmering reflection path.

COMPOSITION: three quarter or full body shot, the landscape around the subject, the light source high in the frame.

AVOID: brush strokes, outlines, smooth gradients, a photo look, signatures, any text.

FORMAT: vertical 2:3, high resolution.
