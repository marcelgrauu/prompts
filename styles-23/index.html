Multiple Exposure

Use the attached photo as the subject. Keep the face, hair, body, pose and outfit exactly as they are, only the style changes.

STYLE: an in camera multiple exposure on film: several exposures of the subject layered on the same frame.

EFFECT: two or three semi transparent copies of the subject overlapping, each slightly shifted, with the head turned to different angles (front, three quarter, profile), the faces ghosting into each other and the eyes sometimes aligning. Optionally one exposure is a landscape ([a misty pine forest] or bare winter trees) that fills the silhouette of the head and body.

COLOUR: [black and white] silver gelatin film with deep blacks and soft greys (or for the colour version: one exposure lit with red light and one with blue on a black background).

TEXTURE AND LIGHT: heavy film grain, soft focus in the ghosted layers, a few dust specks and scratches, light leaks at the edges, slightly uneven development. Soft natural light.

FRAME: optional black film border with frame numbers and small arrows along the edge, like a scanned negative.

COMPOSITION: tight portrait from the chest up, the overlapping heads in the centre, dark or misty background.

AVOID: clean digital blending, perfect symmetry, more than three copies, horror look, brand logos, any other text.

FORMAT: vertical 2:3, high resolution.

Zoom Burst

Use the attached photo as the subject. Keep the face, hair, body, pose and outfit exactly as they are, only the style changes.

STYLE: a zoom burst photograph: the lens zoomed during a long exposure, so everything explodes in streaks from the centre.

EFFECT: the subject sharp in the middle of the frame (face fully in focus), while the background and the edges stretch into straight radial light streaks rushing outwards, stronger and longer towards the corners, like a tunnel of speed.

COLOUR: [city at night]: streaks of neon orange, red, teal, electric blue and white from traffic lights and signs on a dark background (or the black and white version: high contrast monochrome with bright white streaks and deep blacks).

TEXTURE AND LIGHT: sharp detail at the centre, smooth light trails, a bit of grain, strong vanishing point perspective, natural highlights on the subject.

SETTING: [a busy street at night with traffic and tall buildings] (or a long station platform, or an urban tunnel).

COMPOSITION: subject perfectly centred at the vanishing point, chest up or full body, the streaks radiating out from behind the head in every direction.

AVOID: a blurred subject, curved swirl blur, a filter look with no depth, brand logos, readable signs.

FORMAT: vertical 2:3, high resolution.
03 / 07

Panning

Use the attached photo as the subject. Keep the face, hair, body, pose and outfit exactly as they are, only the style changes.

STYLE: a panning photograph: the camera followed the subject during a slow shutter, so the subject is sharp and the background melts into horizontal streaks.

EFFECT: the subject moving from left to right in profile or three quarter, crisp and in focus (face, clothes, hair), with only slight motion softness at the very edges. The whole background stretched into long, smooth horizontal lines of colour and light. Optional: the subject [riding a scooter] (or a motorbike, a bicycle, or simply walking fast).

COLOUR: cinematic street tones: teal and deep blue shadows with warm orange, amber and red lights, a few neon pinks; rich but natural skin.

TEXTURE AND LIGHT: dusk or golden hour light, glowing streaks from shop lights and traffic, slight film grain, shallow depth of field.

SETTING: [a city street at dusk] with road markings, crossing stripes and shop fronts all blurred into lines.

COMPOSITION: subject in the lower or middle third, a bit off centre with space in front of the direction of movement, the streaks filling the rest of the frame.

AVOID: a blurred subject, radial blur, a static sharp background, brand logos on clothes or vehicles, readable signs.

FORMAT: vertical 2:3, high resolution.
04 / 07

Underwater

Use the attached photo as the subject. Keep the face, hair, body, pose and outfit exactly as they are, only the style changes.

STYLE: a fine art underwater photograph shot below the surface of deep clear water, the subject floating weightless in the pose from the photo.

COLOUR: deep teal, turquoise and navy blue water that darkens to almost black at the bottom and the edges. Skin keeps its natural tone, slightly cooled by the blue, with warm highlights only where the sun hits.

LIGHT: sunlight from the surface above. Bright god rays fanning down through the water, caustic light patterns (thin wavy rippling lines of light) moving across the face, shoulders and clothes, and the rippled silver mirror of the surface visible at the top of the frame.

TEXTURE: tiny bubbles escaping from the nose and lips and floating around the body, suspended particles catching the light, hair spreading out and drifting upward in slow strands, loose clothing billowing softly like fabric in slow motion. A little haze and softness with distance, like real water.

SETTING: open water with no floor visible, or the soft blue tiles of a deep pool far below. Optional: a small school of [YELLOW STRIPED FISH] swimming past in the background.

COMPOSITION: choose one: the whole body floating diagonally in the lower half with the light rays above, or a close up of the face tilted up towards the surface, eyes closed, lips relaxed, caustic lines across the skin. Lots of empty blue space around.

AVOID: dry hair, a flat blue filter, goggles or diving gear, a visible studio, brand logos, watermarks, any text.

FORMAT: vertical 2:3, high resolution.
05 / 07

Silhouette

Use the attached photo as the subject. Keep the face, hair, body, pose and outfit exactly as they are, only the style changes.

STYLE: a golden hour backlit silhouette photo, the subject a solid black shape against the setting sun.

COLOUR: the sky in a smooth gradient from burning orange and amber near the horizon to peach, dusty pink and soft violet higher up. The sun is a small glowing white yellow disc. The subject and the ground are pure black with no visible detail.

LIGHT: the sun low on the horizon, directly behind or just beside the subject, so the whole figure is in shadow. A thin warm glow wraps the edges of the hair, and any thin or sheer fabric glows orange where the sun shines through it. A golden streak of reflected sunlight runs across the water towards the camera.

TEXTURE: crisp clean edges on the silhouette, every strand of hair and every finger readable as a shape, soft warm haze in the air, a few thin clouds lit in gold.

SETTING: a calm sea or beach at sunset, wet sand or shallow water reflecting the light, maybe a dark rock in the foreground or distant hills on the horizon.

COMPOSITION: full body silhouette standing or walking at the water's edge, the profile of the face clearly readable, arms and hands making a clear shape (raised, open, or cupped as if holding the sun). Horizon in the lower third, lots of sky.

AVOID: visible facial features or clothing colours, fill flash on the face, grey muddy shadows, an all red image, brand logos, any text.

FORMAT: vertical 2:3, high resolution.
06 / 07

Rim Light

Use the attached photo as the subject. Keep the face, hair, body, pose and outfit exactly as they are, only the style changes.

STYLE: a low key studio portrait lit only by a strong rim light from behind, on a pure black background.

COLOUR: almost everything is deep black. The only colour is the light on the edges: warm golden amber [or cool white for a black and white version]. The shadow side of the skin keeps a faint natural tone.

LIGHT: one or two hard lights placed behind and to the side of the subject. They draw a bright thin outline along the hair, the profile of the forehead, nose, lips and chin, the jaw, the neck and the shoulder line. Hair glows like fine golden threads, with flyaway strands lit one by one. The front of the face falls mostly into shadow, with only a soft hint of the eyes and cheek. Optional: a small warm lens flare where the light peeks past the shoulder.

TEXTURE: sharp photographic detail, fine film grain in the blacks, a perfectly black background with no gradient.

COMPOSITION: head and shoulders, three quarter turn or full profile, face turned slightly away from the light so the outline is strong, plenty of black space around the head.

AVOID: a lit or grey background, front lighting, coloured gels everywhere, a flat silhouette with no edge light, brand logos, any text.

FORMAT: vertical 2:3, high resolution.
07 / 07

Macro

Use the attached photo as the subject. Keep the face, hair, body, pose and outfit exactly as they are, only the style changes.

STYLE: an extreme macro photograph of one eye of the subject, so close that the iris fills most of the frame.

DETAIL: the iris in the subject's real eye colour, shown as a landscape of fibres: radial streaks and small crypts spreading from the pupil like a sunburst, a darker ring around the outer edge, flecks of [AMBER AND GOLD] around the pupil. The pupil is a deep black circle with a crisp edge. A curved catchlight from a window or ring light reflects on the wet cornea.

TEXTURE AND LIGHT: razor thin depth of field: the iris tack sharp, the lashes in front and the skin around melting into soft blur. Individual eyelashes, tiny moisture on the lid line, fine skin texture and pores, a few thin red veins on the white of the eye. Bright soft side light that makes the iris glow and look translucent.

COLOUR: warm tones on the skin and lashes, the iris saturated and luminous, deep shadows around the eye.

COMPOSITION: tight crop on one eye, pupil slightly off centre, part of the brow or lashes entering the frame.

AVOID: the whole face, heavy makeup, a cartoon or painted eye, fake neon iris colours, brand logos, watermarks, any text.

FORMAT: vertical 2:3, high resolution.
