Grainy Gradient

Use the attached photo as the subject. Keep the face, hair, body, pose and outfit exactly as they are, only the style changes.

STYLE: a grainy gradient poster: the subject dissolved into a soft blurred colour gradient with heavy noise and minimal Swiss style typography.

COLOUR: a luminous gradient of [hot red to pink to violet], with orange, electric blue and deep indigo areas. Background off white, pale grey or pale pink.

TEXTURE: heavy grain and noise across the whole poster, strongest on the gradient edges, soft blur everywhere, glowing orb shapes.

SUBJECT: the silhouette and features dissolved in the blur, recognisable by shape and colours, the face only just readable.

TYPE: small clean sans serif in capitals: a title [YOUR WORD] at the top left, a date [28.02] and a few tiny vertical lines of text along one edge, small numbers in the corners [03] [21].

GRAPHICS: thin lines, small crosshairs and one fine circle.

COMPOSITION: lots of empty space, the gradient glow centred over the subject, the type small and pushed to the corners.

AVOID: a sharp photo, hard edges, logos, real names, venue names.

FORMAT: vertical 2:3, high resolution.

Isometric

Use the attached photo as the subject. Keep the face, hair, body, pose and outfit exactly as they are, only the style changes.

STYLE: a detailed isometric illustration: the subject and their surroundings as a small floating isometric diorama, like a cosy game or an editorial illustration.

COLOUR: harmonious flat colours with one clear theme: [teal and lime green], or warm orange and purple, or navy and pink.

TEXTURE AND LIGHT: clean vector shapes with thin dark brown outlines, two or three tone flat shading, light from the top left, small highlights, a subtle grain.

SUBJECT: a small stylised figure of the subject, simplified but with their hair, outfit and colours, standing in the scene.

SETTING: an isometric block of [a little street with a shop and a cafe], cut out of the ground like a slice, with stairs, plants, tiny props, windows, pipes and a small vehicle.

COMPOSITION: the diorama in the centre on a plain colour background, a true isometric angle with no vanishing points, lots of tiny details, empty space around.

AVOID: perspective distortion, photorealism, logos, any text except an optional small title [MY IDEAL HOME].

FORMAT: vertical 2:3, high resolution.

Cel Shaded

Use the attached photo as the subject. Keep the face, hair, body, pose and outfit exactly as they are, only the style changes.

STYLE: a cel shaded 3D character render, like a stylised anime video game: toon shading with flat colours and hard shadow edges.

COLOUR: bold saturated colours taken from the photo, cool blue and purple shadow tones, a warm rim light, subtle iridescent hue shifts.

TEXTURE AND LIGHT: two or three tone shading with crisp edges and no soft gradients, thick dark outlines around the silhouette and thinner inner lines, sharp white highlight shapes on the hair, a bright rim light.

SUBJECT: slightly stylised proportions, big expressive eyes but keeping the person's features, hair as chunky sculpted strands.

SETTING: a plain black or flat colour background, or a simple street rendered in the same toon style.

COMPOSITION: character portrait from the waist up, centred.

AVOID: realistic skin texture, photographic lighting, known characters, logos, any text.

FORMAT: vertical 2:3, high resolution.

Sticker

Use the attached photo as the subject. Keep the face, hair, body, pose and outfit exactly as they are, only the style changes.

STYLE: a die cut vinyl sticker of the subject as a cute bold illustration, photographed as a real object.

COLOUR: cheerful retro pastels: sage green, cream, peach, sky blue, mustard and pink, with black outlines.

TEXTURE AND LIGHT: a thick white border following the silhouette, glossy laminate with soft reflections, one corner slightly peeling to show the backing, a soft drop shadow.

SUBJECT: the subject as a flat cartoon illustration: thick black outlines, flat colours, simple shading, a slightly big head.

TYPE: a short chunky rounded retro lettering phrase as part of the sticker: [TAKE IT EASY].

GRAPHICS: little sparkles, a sunburst and stars around the figure, inside the border.

COMPOSITION: the sticker held between two fingers against [a clear blue sky], or lying on a plain pastel background, centred.

AVOID: photorealism, brand logos, extra text.

FORMAT: vertical 2:3, high resolution.

Matchbox Label

Use the attached photo as the subject. Keep the face, hair, body, pose and outfit exactly as they are, only the style changes.

STYLE: a vintage matchbox label from the middle of the twentieth century, printed in flat spot colours, a bold graphic illustration.

COLOUR: two to four flat inks only, such as navy blue, orange, red and cream, on aged cream paper.

TEXTURE: old lithographic print with slight misregistration, ink spread, small specks, fine engraved hatching for the shadows, faded and foxed paper, worn corners.

SUBJECT: the subject simplified into a bold graphic silhouette with flat shapes and few details, or drawn in fine engraved lines.

TYPE: bold blocky condensed lettering: [YOUR WORD] at the top and [SAFETY MATCHES] at the bottom, plus a small line of print [60 PCS] [No. 1959].

GRAPHICS: a decorative border, ribbon banners, a sunburst, stars and a small flame.

COMPOSITION: a small rectangular label, the subject centred, text at the top and bottom, a border all around.

AVOID: gradients, a photo look, real brand or factory names, modern fonts.

FORMAT: vertical 2:3, high resolution.

Topographic

Use the attached photo as the subject. Keep the face, hair, body, pose and outfit exactly as they are, only the style changes.

STYLE: topographic map art, the subject drawn as if they were a landscape seen from above, made only of contour lines like a hiking map.

LINES: hundreds of thin flowing contour lines that follow the shape of the face, hair, body and clothes like elevation rings. Tight concentric rings on the high points (nose, cheekbones, shoulders, folds of the clothes), wide open spacing on the flat areas. Every few lines one thicker index line. A few small closed ovals like hill tops, one or two dotted trail lines and a winding river line in [BLUE] crossing the background.

COLOUR: choose one version and keep it consistent. [DARK]: black or deep charcoal background with white, pale yellow and lime green lines. Or [MAP]: warm off white paper with brown lines, soft green vegetation patches and a bright blue river.

TEXTURE: crisp printed lines, a faint square map grid behind everything, slight paper grain, gentle shaded relief so the shapes feel three dimensional.

TYPE: tiny map labels only: coordinates in the corners like [41°34'N 1°49'E] (invented), a small elevation number next to the highest point, a thin scale bar at the bottom.

COMPOSITION: subject from the chest up or three quarter, centred and large, the contour lines continuing outward into the background so the person melts into the terrain.

AVOID: a normal photo with lines on top, realistic skin, real place names, brand logos.

FORMAT: vertical 2:3, high resolution.
07 / 07

Mirror

Use the attached photo as the subject. Keep the face, hair, body, pose and outfit exactly as they are, only the style changes.

STYLE: a surreal mirror symmetry photo, an editorial fashion image where the subject is reflected perfectly in a mirror or glossy surface.

EFFECT: the frame is split by one clean mirror line, [HORIZONTAL] (the subject above, the upside down reflection below, like resting on a polished mirror table) or vertical (the subject facing their own reflection). The reflection is a perfect copy, same pose and outfit, flipped exactly. The seam is sharp and straight, with only a faint line of light where the mirror edge is.

COLOUR: clean, saturated editorial colours. A soft plain background in [LIGHT GREY] or a vivid clear sky blue, with one strong accent colour taken from the photo (red lips, an orange jacket, red shoes) repeated in the reflection.

LIGHT: soft even studio light or bright daylight, crisp shadows, glossy reflections on the mirror surface.

TEXTURE: sharp high end photography, fine film grain, smooth skin with real pores, no blur on the reflection.

COMPOSITION: centred and balanced so the two halves read as one graphic shape, plenty of empty space around, the mirror line crossing the middle of the frame.

AVOID: a kaleidoscope with many copies, warped or melted reflections, double exposure, text, brand logos.

FORMAT: vertical 2:3, high resolution.
