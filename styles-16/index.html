Inflated 3D

Use the attached photo as the subject. Keep the face, hair, body, pose and outfit exactly as they are, only the style changes.

STYLE: an inflated 3D render, the subject and their clothes turned into a puffy object filled with air, like a balloon, a pool float or a quilted puffer jacket.

MATERIAL: every shape is swollen and rounded with soft bulging volumes. The clothes are divided into pillow segments by stitched seams and small creases where the material is pulled tight. Choose one finish: glossy vinyl, soft satin, metallic silver foil or translucent candy jelly. Face and hands are also smooth and puffy, but the features stay recognisable.

COLOUR: one bold main colour from the outfit pushed to a clean saturated tone ([LAVENDER PURPLE], silver, sunny yellow), or a playful rainbow of candy colours (pink, orange, lime, violet) flowing into each other.

LIGHT: soft studio lighting with big bright highlights on every bulge, gentle reflections, a soft contact shadow under the figure. Translucent parts glow with coloured light passing through.

BACKGROUND: a plain seamless studio backdrop in one flat colour: [SOFT LILAC], light grey, deep purple or white.

COMPOSITION: full body or three quarter, centred, the figure floating or standing on the floor with lots of clean space around it, like a product shot.

AVOID: a real photo, wrinkled fabric texture, sharp edges, text, brand logos.

FORMAT: vertical 2:3, high resolution.

Glassmorphism

Use the attached photo as the subject. Keep the face, hair, body, pose and outfit exactly as they are, only the style changes.

STYLE: glassmorphism, a modern interface poster where the subject is seen through frosted glass panels floating over soft glowing colour.

GLASS: one or two big rounded rectangle panels of frosted glass in front of the subject, with a thin bright edge, a subtle inner glow and a soft drop shadow. Everything behind the glass is blurred and softened, the parts outside the panels stay sharp. Optionally one area of ribbed or grid glass that breaks the image into small refracted squares.

COLOUR: blurry glowing gradient orbs behind the subject in [ORANGE, CORAL AND HOT PINK] (or electric blue and violet), on a muted background of dusty lilac, grey or teal. Colours bleed and glow through the glass.

LIGHT: soft and diffuse, light catching the glass edges, gentle reflections.

INTERFACE: minimal UI elements on the glass in thin white sans serif: a small heading [YOUR WORD], one short line of tiny body text, a round icon with an arrow, a pill shaped button with [JOIN], and a small number like 024 in a corner.

COMPOSITION: subject centred and big, the main glass card overlapping the body, lots of breathing space and a clean grid.

AVOID: hard plastic look, dark heavy shadows, cluttered text, brand logos, app names.

FORMAT: vertical 2:3, high resolution.

Iridescent

Use the attached photo as the subject. Keep the face, hair, body, pose and outfit exactly as they are, only the style changes.

STYLE: an iridescent 3D render, as if the subject were made of liquid holographic chrome and glass, shot in a clean studio.

MATERIAL: the clothes, hair and skin highlights turn into a smooth pearly surface with an oil slick sheen. Rainbow colour shifts flow across every curve: pink, violet, cyan, mint green and soft gold, changing with the angle. Some edges look like clear glass that refracts the light. The face stays recognisable with pearly highlights.

COLOUR: mostly silver and white with the rainbow shimmer on top, on a background of soft light grey (or deep black for a more dramatic version: [GREY]).

LIGHT: bright soft studio light from above and the side, crisp specular highlights, rainbow caustics and coloured light spilling onto the floor, a soft reflection under the subject.

TEXTURE: perfectly smooth and glossy, a few tiny sparkles where the light hits hardest, no grain.

TYPE: optional, very minimal: one lowercase word in clean white sans serif across the middle, [shimmer.], and one tiny line of mono caption text in a corner.

COMPOSITION: subject centred, three quarter or from the waist up, floating in empty space like a premium product shot.

AVOID: a flat rainbow filter, neon cartoon colours, heavy text, brand logos, app or software names.

FORMAT: vertical 2:3, high resolution.

Paper Craft

Use the attached photo as the subject. Keep the face, hair, body, pose and outfit exactly as they are, only the style changes.

STYLE: a low poly papercraft model, the subject built as a handmade paper sculpture from flat folded triangles and polygons.

MATERIAL: the whole figure (face, hair, clothes, shoes) is made of matte card paper folded into sharp facets. Visible fold lines, slightly rounded paper edges, a few small glue tabs and seams where pieces join. Each facet catches the light differently, so the shape reads through light and shadow, not drawing.

COLOUR: soft paper tones: [WHITE AND CREAM] or kraft beige, with the outfit's main colours kept as flat coloured paper (for example orange and teal). No gradients inside a facet.

LIGHT: soft studio light from one side, crisp shading between the faces of the polygons, a clean contact shadow on the ground.

SETTING: the model stands on a simple flat paper or card base, on a seamless background in light grey, pale blue grey or dark charcoal.

COMPOSITION: full body, centred, three quarter view so the folds and volumes are clear, plenty of empty space around like a product photo of a sculpture.

AVOID: smooth 3D skin, realistic textures, photo elements, text, logos, watermarks.

FORMAT: vertical 2:3, high resolution.

Miniature

Use the attached photo as the subject. Keep the face, hair, body, pose and outfit exactly as they are, only the style changes.

STYLE: a handmade miniature diorama, the subject as a tiny hand painted figurine inside a detailed scale model, photographed on a table.

FIGURE: the subject as a small painted resin figure, around 3 cm tall, same pose and outfit, with the slightly simplified painted look of a real miniature.

SET: a small scene built on a thick wooden base: [A COSY CABIN WITH A PORCH] (or a tiny street corner, an old van covered in moss, a ruined arch). Full of tiny details: moss and grass tufts, little flowers, miniature trees, a pond made of glossy resin, string lights, small furniture, wood and stone textures, weathering and rust.

LIGHT: warm interior lights glowing in the windows, soft daylight from a window on the side, gentle shadows.

CAMERA: macro lens, very shallow depth of field, the figure in sharp focus and the edges of the model blurred, so it clearly reads as small. Optionally one real hand at the edge of the frame holding the base for scale.

SETTING: the diorama sits on a wooden workbench or desk, with a soft out of focus room behind.

COMPOSITION: three quarter view from slightly above, the base fully visible, the figure placed on a third.

AVOID: life size scene, video game render look, text, signatures, brand logos.

FORMAT: vertical 2:3, high resolution.

Plush Toy

Use the attached photo as the subject. Keep the face, hair, body, pose and outfit exactly as they are, only the style changes.

STYLE: a handmade plush toy, the subject turned into a soft stuffed toy version of themselves.

SHAPE: cute toy proportions: an oversized round head, small soft body, short stubby arms and legs, everything slightly squishy and padded. Keep the hairstyle, hair colour and outfit, but sewn from fabric pieces. Eyes as shiny black safety buttons or embroidered ovals, a small embroidered nose and mouth, maybe soft pink felt cheeks.

MATERIAL: real fabric: soft minky plush, curly boucle or long fluffy faux fur for the hair, felt patches for details. Visible stitched seams where the panels meet, a few tiny stray fibres, a small ribbon or appliqué detail ([A STAR]).

COLOUR: soft pastel version of the outfit colours: baby blue, blush pink, butter yellow, cream, with one darker accent.

LIGHT: soft warm daylight, gentle shadows, fabric fuzz glowing at the edges.

SETTING: the toy sits on a white shelf or plain light surface against a soft pastel wall ([PEACH]), like a product photo of a real handmade toy.

COMPOSITION: full body, centred, eye level, plenty of space around.

AVOID: plastic or 3D render shine, a human in a costume, existing characters, text, labels, brand logos.

FORMAT: vertical 2:3, high resolution.

Marble Statue

Use the attached photo as the subject. Keep the face, hair, body, pose and outfit exactly as they are, only the style changes.

STYLE: a classical white marble statue, the subject carved in stone by a master sculptor, photographed in a museum.

MATERIAL: polished pure white statuary marble with very soft grey veins, subtle translucency on the thin edges, tiny pores and chisel marks in the hollows. Eyes carved blank without pupils, hair turned into deep carved curls or waves. The outfit is carved as flowing drapery with deep sculpted folds, or as a thin veil of stone that clings to the face and body and lets the features show through.

COLOUR: almost monochrome: ivory, cream and cool grey, maybe a warm hint of aged stone.

LIGHT: dramatic museum lighting from one side and above, deep soft shadows in the folds, bright highlights on cheekbones, nose and shoulders.

BACKGROUND: plain deep black or dark charcoal grey, nothing else. Optionally the statue stands on a simple block pedestal of veined marble.

COMPOSITION: either a bust from the chest up or a full figure in the same pose as the photo, centred, low camera angle to feel monumental.

AVOID: grey plastic, cracked ruins, coloured paint, text, plaques, real artwork names.

FORMAT: vertical 2:3, high resolution.
