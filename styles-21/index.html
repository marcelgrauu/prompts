Line Art

Use the attached photo as the subject. Keep the face, hair, body, pose and outfit exactly as they are, only the style changes.

STYLE: minimalist continuous line art, the subject drawn with one thin flowing line that hardly ever lifts from the paper.

COLOUR: pure black line on clean white paper (or on warm cream [#F3E9E0]). Nothing else.

LINE: a fine, even, confident line, smooth curves, long loops for the hair that sweep beyond the head, the face reduced to the essentials: eyebrows, closed or open eyes as small curves, the nose drawn in one stroke with the brow, a simple outline of the lips, the jaw and neck flowing into the shoulders. A few overlapping strokes where the line crosses itself. Optionally one slender leafy branch drawn with the same line growing beside the face.

TEXTURE: crisp ink on smooth paper, no shading, no fill, no grey wash.

COMPOSITION: head and shoulders, lots of empty white space around, the drawing slightly off centre, the line fading out at the bottom.

AVOID: colour, sketchy hatching, multiple line weights, realistic shading, signatures, any text.

FORMAT: vertical 2:3, high resolution.

Flat Illustration

Use the attached photo as the subject. Keep the face, hair, body, pose and outfit exactly as they are, only the style changes.

STYLE: a modern editorial flat illustration, like a magazine or poster illustration made by a contemporary illustrator.

COLOUR: a limited palette of four or five colours: one bold background colour ([cobalt blue], or tomato red, or sage grey), plus warm skin tones, black, white and one accent ([orange]).

SHAPES: the subject simplified into clean flat shapes with no outlines: small head, long limbs, simple dot eyes or minimal features, hair as one solid shape, clothes as big blocks of colour with a few graphic folds. Props and background reduced to simple shapes: plants, lamps, windows, furniture, a street.

TEXTURE AND LIGHT: soft grain and noise shading, subtle gradients inside the shapes, a strong light source with long flat shadows or warm window light cutting into a cool room.

SETTING: [a city window looking over a sunny street] (or a calm living room at dusk, or a busy desk).

TYPE: optional four short neutral words in a small bold serif, one in each corner of the poster: [Hello] [there] [good] [days].

COMPOSITION: clear and graphic, subject large in the frame, plenty of solid background colour around.

AVOID: 3D rendering, realistic faces, thick black outlines, gradients that look glossy, brand logos.

FORMAT: vertical 2:3, high resolution.

Storybook

Use the attached photo as the subject. Keep the face, hair, body, pose and outfit exactly as they are, only the style changes.

STYLE: a children's picture book illustration, warm and whimsical, painted in gouache and watercolour.

COLOUR: warm and friendly: poppy red, mustard yellow, sage and leaf green, sky blue, soft browns and cream, rosy pink cheeks.

CHARACTER: the subject turned into the main character of the story: simplified round face, small dot or bead eyes, rosy cheeks, gentle smile, hairstyle and outfit kept recognisable but softened.

TEXTURE AND LIGHT: visible gouache brush marks, watercolour blooms and soft edges, coloured pencil details, textured cold press paper showing through. Soft daylight, gentle shadows.

SETTING: a cosy whimsical scene with small magical details: [sitting on a giant snail reading a book] (or on a stone bench beside a green door with a cat, or on a hill with friendly animals and a picnic, or a little boat on curly waves). Mushrooms, flowers, leaves, birds and bugs scattered around.

COMPOSITION: subject as the clear focus in the middle, framed by plants and props, a page from a picture book with room around the edges.

AVOID: 3D render look, sharp realistic detail, dark mood, known characters, brand logos, any text.

FORMAT: vertical 2:3, high resolution.

Botanical

Use the attached photo as the subject. Keep the face, hair, body, pose and outfit exactly as they are, only the style changes.

STYLE: an antique botanical illustration plate from an old natural history book, a hand coloured engraving.

COLOUR: soft natural watercolour tones over fine black engraved lines: leaf greens, berry reds and purples, citrus orange, creamy white petals, all slightly faded on aged ivory paper.

SUBJECT: the person drawn in the same technique, fine engraved contour lines and cross hatching with delicate watercolour wash, from the chest up, framed or entwined by detailed plant specimens: [magnolia, blackberry branch, lemon with a cut slice, thistle and daisy], with roots, buds, seeds and cross sections.

TEXTURE: aged paper with foxing, soft yellowed edges, a faint plate mark, slight ink bleed.

TYPE: small italic serif labels in the style of the old plates: an invented Latin name under the subject such as [Homo elegans L.], small figure numbers next to each plant (1, 2a, 2b), a plate number top right PLATE XLVI and a short caption line at the bottom in small caps.

COMPOSITION: centred specimen layout with plenty of empty paper around, plants arranged symmetrically around the subject like a scientific plate.

AVOID: modern digital look, saturated colours, photographic skin, real book titles, real names or signatures.

FORMAT: vertical 2:3, high resolution.

Tattoo Flash

Use the attached photo as the subject. Keep the face, hair, body, pose and outfit exactly as they are, only the style changes.

STYLE: an old school traditional tattoo flash sheet, the subject drawn as the main tattoo design.

COLOUR: limited classic tattoo palette: solid black, blood red, mustard yellow, olive and emerald green, teal blue and a little skin peach. Colours flat and bold.

LINE: very thick, clean black outlines, solid black shadow areas, whip shading and stippled dot gradients inside the colours, small white highlights.

SUBJECT: the person simplified into a bold tattoo portrait from the chest up, strong outlines around the face, hair as chunky shapes, a banner scroll across the bottom with [YOUR NAME].

GRAPHICS: smaller flash designs around it: a dagger through a rose, a swallow, a jester hat skull, an open mouthed crocodile, a koi fish, a blueberry with flowers, stars, sparkles and little crosses filling the gaps.

TEXTURE: printed on aged tan parchment paper with stains and soft edges, like a sheet pinned on a tattoo shop wall.

COMPOSITION: main design big in the centre, flash pieces arranged around it with breathing space, like a real flash page.

AVOID: realistic shading, fine line minimal tattoos, photo of skin, real studio names or logos, other text.

FORMAT: vertical 2:3, high resolution.

Graffiti

Use the attached photo as the subject. Keep the face, hair, body, pose and outfit exactly as they are, only the style changes.

STYLE: a big spray painted graffiti mural of the subject, photographed on a real wall in the street.

COLOUR: loud and saturated: orange, hot pink, cyan, lemon yellow, lime green, purple and white, with thick black outlines.

TECHNIQUE: the subject painted in bold cartoon street art style with chunky black outlines, flat colour fills, simple highlights and soft spray fades. Visible overspray, paint drips running down, small tags and scribbles in the background, a few crowns, stars, eyes and sunburst rays around the figure.

TYPE: big wildstyle graffiti letters behind or beside the subject, interlocking with arrows and 3D blocks, reading [FRESH].

SETTING: a concrete or brick wall on a sunny street corner, a bit of sky and building above, asphalt and pavement in front, a drop cloth with spray cans and paint buckets on the ground.

TEXTURE AND LIGHT: natural daylight on the wall, rough concrete texture showing through the paint, slight wear and peeling.

COMPOSITION: wall seen straight on or at a slight angle, the painted subject huge and filling most of the wall, a little street context at the edges for scale.

AVOID: clean digital vector look, real crew names or tags, real event names, brand logos, profanity.

FORMAT: vertical 2:3, high resolution.
07 / 07

Doodle

Use the attached photo as the subject. Keep the face, hair, body, pose and outfit exactly as they are, only the style changes.

STYLE: a black ink doodle art piece, the subject drawn and filled with hundreds of small hand drawn doodles.

COLOUR: black fineliner on white paper. Optional single accent: one small shape in [red] (like a heart on the chest).

SUBJECT: the outline of the person drawn with a bolder, confident marker line so the silhouette and face read clearly. Inside and around the figure, the space is packed with tiny doodles: cute little monsters with big eyes and open mouths, faces, stars, hearts, flowers, clouds, squiggles, arrows, tiny houses and city buildings, wavy line bands and dotted textures.

TEXTURE: real ink on paper, slightly uneven pen pressure, small wobbles in the lines, no shading except hatching and dot patterns.

COMPOSITION: dense all over pattern filling the whole page edge to edge, the face kept a little cleaner and simpler so it stays recognisable, the doodles getting smaller towards the edges.

AVOID: colour fills, grey tones, 3D, known cartoon characters, readable words, signatures or watermarks.

FORMAT: vertical 2:3, high resolution.
