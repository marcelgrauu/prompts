Knitted

Use the attached photo as the subject. Keep the face, hair, body, pose and outfit exactly as they are, only the style changes.

STYLE: a handmade textile artwork: the subject and the scene entirely knitted and crocheted from yarn, like a fibre art piece photographed in a studio.

COLOUR: the colours of the photo translated into yarn: cosy tones like denim blue, rust orange, mustard, moss green, cream and dusty pink, with a few bright accents.

TEXTURE AND LIGHT: visible V shaped knit stitches, chunky chain stitches, purl ridges, a fuzzy mohair halo, thick and thin art yarn with flecks of colour, small knots, bobbles and pompoms, knitted tubes, loose ends and fringes hanging down. Soft diffused daylight and gentle shadows that show the depth of the stitches.

SUBJECT: the face knitted with finer yarn and small stitches so it stays recognisable, eyes, nose and mouth embroidered on top, hair as thick loops and fringes, clothes in different stitch patterns.

SETTING: [a street] knitted too, with stripes of different yarns, uneven rows, crochet textures for trees, sky and clouds.

COMPOSITION: the piece shown flat like a tapestry against a white wall or hanging outdoors among trees, full figure, irregular edges with loose yarn ends.

AVOID: printed fabric, smooth CGI, a plastic look, logos, watermarks, any text.

FORMAT: vertical 2:3, high resolution.

Origami

Use the attached photo as the subject. Keep the face, hair, body, pose and outfit exactly as they are, only the style changes.

STYLE: a paper craft scene: the subject and everything around folded from paper (origami), photographed as a real miniature on a table.

COLOUR: soft paper colours: cream and white for the figure, pastel pink, sky blue and lilac, plus patterned papers with small floral prints in red, teal and gold. The colours of the outfit translated into coloured papers.

TEXTURE AND LIGHT: crisp sharp creases, flat triangular facets, layered pleats, a fine paper fibre grain, edges catching the light. Soft natural window light from one side, warm and gentle, long soft shadows, shallow depth of field.

SUBJECT: the person folded in a faceted geometric way, the face built from flat planes but still recognisable, hair as folded pleats, clothes as overlapping paper panels.

SETTING: on a wooden table near a bright window, with small folded cranes, flowers and leaves around, the background softly blurred.

COMPOSITION: full figure, centred, camera at table height, creamy bokeh behind.

AVOID: a smooth 3D render, a cardboard look, glue or tape, logos, any text.

FORMAT: vertical 2:3, high resolution.

Brick Toy

Use the attached photo as the subject. Keep the face, hair, body, pose and outfit exactly as they are, only the style changes.

STYLE: toy photography: the subject turned into a small plastic building brick figure, shot with a macro lens in a real or brick built scene.

COLOUR: bright glossy plastic: yellow skin, primary red, blue and green, black, plus the colours of the outfit translated into plastic.

TEXTURE AND LIGHT: shiny moulded plastic with soft reflections, tiny studs on top of every brick, the face and clothes printed on the figure, simple claw hands. Cinematic light: warm golden sunlight or a single lamp, glowing practical lights, strong backlight, very shallow depth of field with creamy bokeh.

SUBJECT: a blocky figure with a cylindrical head and a printed face matching the person's expression, a hair piece shaped like their hairstyle, the outfit printed on the torso and legs.

SETTING: [a city street at golden hour] built from studded bricks, or the figure placed in the real world at miniature scale: against a brick wall, paddling a tiny boat on a pond, sitting at a window with a cup of coffee.

COMPOSITION: camera low at the figure's eye level, the figure small in the frame with plenty of environment, blurred foreground.

AVOID: toy brand logos on studs or boxes, known characters, a cartoon render, any text.

FORMAT: vertical 2:3, high resolution.

Chalk Art

Use the attached photo as the subject. Keep the face, hair, body, pose and outfit exactly as they are, only the style changes.

STYLE: a sidewalk chalk drawing: the subject drawn with coloured chalk directly on the ground, photographed from above.

COLOUR: bright pastel chalk: pink, lilac, sky blue, mint, lemon yellow, orange and white, glowing against dark grey asphalt or light concrete.

TEXTURE AND LIGHT: dusty powdery strokes with the bumpy ground showing through, areas blended with the fingers, bold outlines, white highlights, cracks and seams of the pavement crossing the drawing. Flat daylight, maybe the shadow of a person falling on one edge.

SUBJECT: drawn simply but recognisable, filled with glowing colour, outlined in white.

GRAPHICS: starbursts with soft rainbow glows, planets, a rocket, small stars and dots around the figure. Alternative: koi fish swimming in a chalk pond.

PROPS: a few broken chalk sticks and coloured dust at the edge of the drawing, the edge of a curb or a strip of grass.

COMPOSITION: top down view of the pavement, the drawing filling most of the frame.

AVOID: paper texture, clean digital lines, logos, any text.

FORMAT: vertical 2:3, high resolution.

Crayon

Use the attached photo as the subject. Keep the face, hair, body, pose and outfit exactly as they are, only the style changes.

STYLE: a wax crayon and oil pastel drawing on paper, loose and joyful, like a page from an illustrator's sketchbook.

COLOUR: bright saturated crayon colours: tomato red, cobalt blue, emerald, lime, sunny yellow, orange and pink, on white or warm cream paper.

TEXTURE: visible waxy strokes, short parallel hatching in different directions, paper grain showing through, colours layered on top of each other, scribbled fills going a bit outside the lines, wobbly outlines in a contrasting colour.

SUBJECT: drawn simply, slightly naive but recognisable, outlined in coloured line, flat fields of colour, shadows as blue or purple scribbles.

SETTING: [a sunny garden], or a table with fruit, a jug and a vase of flowers, drawn with the same crayons.

COMPOSITION: subject centred, a margin of white paper around, light unfinished edges.

AVOID: smooth digital colour, gradients, photorealism, signatures, any text.

FORMAT: vertical 2:3, high resolution.

Felt

Use the attached photo as the subject. Keep the face, hair, body, pose and outfit exactly as they are, only the style changes.

STYLE: a handmade felt craft scene: the subject and the background cut from felt sheets and stitched together, photographed as a real object.

COLOUR: soft saturated felt colours: mustard, tomato red, teal, leaf green, warm orange, pink and cream, plus the colours of the outfit.

TEXTURE AND LIGHT: fuzzy matte felt with tiny fibres, visible running stitch and blanket stitch along the edges in contrasting embroidery thread, small decorative stitches, layered pieces with a little padding so they look puffy, zigzag pinked edges. Soft natural daylight and gentle shadows.

SUBJECT: the face made of flat felt pieces with eyes, nose and mouth embroidered in thread, rosy felt cheeks, hair cut from felt with stitched lines.

SETTING: on a rustic wooden table or a gingham cloth, with spools of thread, small scissors, a cup of tea and little felt stars or leaves around. Alternative: the whole scene as a felt landscape inside a wooden frame on a wall.

COMPOSITION: overhead or slightly angled view, the figure centred, props at the edges.

AVOID: printed fabric, plastic, a 3D render, logos, watermarks, any text.

FORMAT: vertical 2:3, high resolution.

Balloon

Use the attached photo as the subject. Keep the face, hair, body, pose and outfit exactly as they are, only the style changes.

STYLE: a balloon art sculpture: the subject made entirely of twisted modelling balloons, photographed in a studio.

COLOUR: glossy candy colours: magenta, pink, yellow, mint green, white, orange and purple, taken from the outfit. Alternative: one single colour in mirror chrome, such as [MAGENTA].

TEXTURE AND LIGHT: shiny latex with strong white highlights, smooth inflated tubes twisted into segments, tight pinched twists at every joint, small knotted tips at the ends, slightly translucent where the rubber is thin. Clean soft studio light and a soft reflection on the floor.

SUBJECT: the body built from long twisted balloon segments, the head a round balloon with simple features, the hair and clothes in matching balloon colours so the person is still recognisable.

SETTING: a plain seamless white or light grey background, maybe a few loose balloons on the floor.

COMPOSITION: full figure, centred, slightly low camera angle.

AVOID: real skin, fabric, drawn outlines, logos, any text.

FORMAT: vertical 2:3, high resolution.
