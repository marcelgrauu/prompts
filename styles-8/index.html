Bauhaus

Use the attached photo as the subject. Keep the face, hair, body, pose and outfit exactly as they are, only the style changes.

STYLE: a Bauhaus style exhibition poster from the 1920s, reduced to geometry, grid and type, combined with a cut out photo of the subject.

COLOUR: primary red, yellow and blue with black, on warm off white or cream paper. Alternative palettes: deep forest green, mint and black, or dark teal with one coral accent. The subject's photo in black and white or duotone.

TEXTURE: flat matte screen printed colour, very subtle paper grain, clean edges, slightly aged paper.

GRAPHICS: bold geometric shapes on a strict grid: a full circle, concentric rings, half circles, squares, a triangle, thick black bars and rows of vertical stripes. Shapes interact with the photo: a red circle behind the head, black stripes across the base, a yellow block beside the body.

TYPE: a geometric or condensed sans serif headline in capitals at the top, widely spaced: [YOUR WORD]. A large year on the opposite side: [1923]. Two or three small lines of info text at the bottom: [EXHIBITION], [JULY 19], [THE FOUNDATION OF MODERN DESIGN].

COMPOSITION: asymmetric but balanced grid layout, a thin rule under the headline, the subject cut out from the waist up in the middle, lots of empty cream space around the shapes.

AVOID: gradients, 3D effects, ornaments, drop shadows, brand logos, any other text.

FORMAT: vertical 2:3, high resolution.

Swiss Poster

Use the attached photo as the subject. Keep the face, hair, body, pose and outfit exactly as they are, only the style changes.

STYLE: an International Typographic Style poster, the classic grid poster of the 1950s and 60s: strict grid, huge sans serif type and a bold photo of the subject.

COLOUR: two or three colours only: black, white or light grey, plus one strong accent: [RED] (or an acid yellow background, or ultramarine blue). The photo in black and white or in a single duotone.

TYPE: an enormous neo grotesque sans serif, flush left, tight leading and tight letter spacing, letters so big they crop at the edges of the poster: [YOUR WORD]. Small text in the same family set in neat aligned columns: [friday], [february 21], [8:00 pm / all ages], [the summit hall]. A small number in a corner like [01].

GRAPHICS: one or two solid rectangles in the accent colour, a plus sign or a small grid of dots, thin hairline rules separating the columns.

TEXTURE: clean offset print, flat ink, very subtle paper texture.

COMPOSITION: mathematical grid with clear columns and generous white space, the subject's photo cropped boldly (chest up or tight on the face) sitting in one module of the grid, type overlapping or interlocking with the photo, asymmetric layout aligned to the left.

AVOID: centred layouts, decorative fonts, gradients, illustrations, brand logos, real band or venue names.

FORMAT: vertical 2:3, high resolution.

Art Deco

Use the attached photo as the subject. Keep the face, hair, body, pose and outfit exactly as they are, only the style changes.

STYLE: an Art Deco travel and event poster from the late 1920s and 1930s, elegant, streamlined and geometric.

COLOUR: limited lithographic palette: black, deep teal or midnight blue, metallic gold, ochre and cream, plus one accent (coral red or jade green). Smooth airbrushed gradients inside the shapes.

TEXTURE: lithograph print on slightly yellowed paper, soft airbrush gradients, fine grain, clean sharp edges, a little ageing around the borders.

SUBJECT: the subject stylised into long, elegant streamlined shapes: sleek geometric hair waves, almond eyes, defined lips, elongated neck and hands, clothes turned into flowing simple planes with a few long highlight lines. Optional accessories: long gloves, a pearl necklace, bangles.

GRAPHICS: radiating sunburst rays or searchlight beams behind the subject, stepped skyscrapers, speed lines, chevrons, fan motifs, stylised waves, a streamlined locomotive or ocean liner in the background.

TYPE: a tall thin Deco display typeface with geometric letters, centred at the top or bottom: [YOUR WORD]. One small line of supporting text in widely spaced capitals: [JUNE 21] or [GO BY TRAIN]. A small invented artist signature in a corner.

COMPOSITION: symmetrical, vertical and monumental, subject central and seen slightly from below, rays converging behind the head, a thin decorative border.

AVOID: photographic realism, Victorian ornament, real places, company names, brand logos, extra text.

FORMAT: vertical 2:3, high resolution.

Zine Collage

Use the attached photo as the subject. Keep the face, hair, body, pose and outfit exactly as they are, only the style changes.

STYLE: a punk zine collage page, cut and pasted by hand and run through a photocopier, raw and DIY.

COLOUR: mostly black and white photocopy, with one or two loud accents: hot pink, red marker, or pink lace paper. Grey and off white paper scraps.

TEXTURE: harsh photocopier contrast with crushed blacks, toner noise, speckles and streaks, torn and scissor cut paper edges, layers of masking tape and clear tape, staples, creases, glue stains, photocopied newspaper columns filling the background.

SUBJECT: the subject's photo photocopied in harsh black and white and cut out roughly, optionally a black bar over the eyes, with red marker scribbles over and around it (spikes, crown, stars, circles).

TYPE: ransom note lettering cut from magazines in mixed fonts, sizes and colours spelling a short word: [YOUR WORD]. Stencil and typewriter text strips and cut out labels with neutral words like [ISSUE TWO], [THINK], [NO RULES], [DO IT YOURSELF], plus small unreadable newspaper text.

GRAPHICS: doodled stars, arrows, X marks, spirals and scribbled underlines, a few stickers, a hand drawn star outline, a halftone cut out of an old telephone or cassette.

COMPOSITION: crowded and chaotic but still readable, subject big in the centre, text pieces stuck at angles around the edges.

AVOID: clean digital layout, neat alignment, real band names, swear words, political symbols, brand logos.

FORMAT: vertical 2:3, high resolution.

Constructivism

Use the attached photo as the subject. Keep the face, hair, body, pose and outfit exactly as they are, only the style changes.

STYLE: a 1920s constructivist poster, powerful and dynamic, built from photomontage and bold geometric blocks.

COLOUR: only red, black and aged cream paper, with the subject's photo in high contrast black and white or grey.

TEXTURE: flat lithographic ink, slightly faded red, aged paper grain, small scuffs and fold marks.

GRAPHICS: sharp diagonals everywhere: thick red and black beams, wedges and stripes crossing the frame at 30 to 45 degrees, a large red circle behind the head, rays radiating from one corner, a megaphone or lightning bolt shape, simple factory, tower or gear silhouettes.

TYPE: big heavy condensed block capitals with square geometric letters, set at an angle along the diagonals, in black, red, or white on red: [YOUR WORD]. A second shorter line in smaller blocks: [FORWARD]. Text broken over several lines and rotated.

COMPOSITION: dynamic diagonal layout, the subject as a cut out photo from the chest up, seen from below or in strong profile, placed off centre and overlapping the red circle, beams and type crossing behind and in front.

AVOID: soft gradients, ornamental decoration, flags, political symbols, brand logos, real names.

FORMAT: vertical 2:3, high resolution.
06 / 07

Psychedelic

Use the attached photo as the subject. Keep the face, hair, body, pose and outfit exactly as they are, only the style changes.

STYLE: a late 1960s psychedelic concert poster, hand drawn, with melting lettering that flows like liquid around the subject.

COLOUR: vibrating complementary colours side by side so they buzz: hot red and electric cyan, orange and violet, magenta and lime green, pink and orange. Two or three flat inks plus black or cream.

TEXTURE: screen printed poster, flat inks, slight paper grain, thin outlines around every shape.

SUBJECT: face drawn in clean ink lines with flat colour, heavy eye makeup and stylised lips, hair turned into long flowing waves that keep going into the background.

TYPE: swollen, bubbly hand drawn lettering that warps to fill the shapes, letters bending around the head and hair: [YOUR WORD]. A few lines of event text in the same melting style along the curves: [SATURDAY APRIL 28], [THE GARDEN HALL], [8 PM]. Hard to read at first glance on purpose.

GRAPHICS: flowing ribbons and wavy contour lines filling the whole background, concentric swirls, art nouveau flowers and petals, daisies, eyes, sun rays, stars, a decorative patterned border.

COMPOSITION: subject centred, every inch filled with pattern and lettering, no empty space, everything swirling around the face.

AVOID: photographic texture, 3D, gradients, real band or venue names, brand logos.

FORMAT: vertical 2:3, high resolution.

Brutalist

Use the attached photo as the subject. Keep the face, hair, body, pose and outfit exactly as they are, only the style changes.

STYLE: a brutalist graphic design poster: raw, heavy and typographic, deliberately unpolished, mixing concrete architecture with bold type.

COLOUR: mostly black, white and concrete grey, with at most one loud accent: [SALMON RED] (or acid green). The photo in high contrast black and white.

TEXTURE: rough concrete with pores and stains, photocopy grain, halftone patches, slight scan noise, a pale grey paper sheet.

TYPE: an enormous heavy grotesk or extended black sans serif, so big it breaks into blocks of two or three letters per line: [YOUR WORD]. Tiny monospaced captions in columns, issue numbers like [004], labels like [MONO 009], a barcode, arrows and slashes.

GRAPHICS: visible grid lines and frames, harsh boxes around elements, crude underlines, thin outlined shapes and curves crossing the layout, a small starburst, a row of small icons.

SUBJECT: the subject in a harsh black and white photo, cut abruptly by the frame edge, placed in front of or next to a massive concrete building with deep shadows and repeated balconies.

COMPOSITION: top heavy dense type, raw asymmetric layout on a pale grey background, nothing decorative, strong but crude.

AVOID: soft gradients, pretty serif fonts, anime characters, real names, readable QR codes, brand logos.

FORMAT: vertical 2:3, high resolution.
