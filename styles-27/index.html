Cottagecore

Use the attached photo as the subject. Keep the face, hair, body, pose and outfit exactly as they are, only the style changes.

STYLE: a cottagecore aesthetic photo, slow countryside life captured on warm film.

COLOUR: honey gold, moss green, cream linen and soft berry red. Warm late afternoon light, nothing cold or neon.

TEXTURE AND LIGHT: low golden sun through grass and trees, soft haze and gentle lens bloom, fine film grain, slightly faded blacks.

SCENE AND PROPS: the subject in a wildflower meadow or orchard, linen or cotton clothes, a woven wicker basket with strawberries, an open book or a picnic blanket, daisies and tall grass in the foreground.

COMPOSITION: candid and unposed, as if caught in the moment, subject off centre with plenty of nature around, shallow depth of field with flowers blurred in front.

AVOID: modern objects, phones, cars, logos, harsh flash, saturated digital colours, any text.

FORMAT: vertical 2:3, high resolution.

Dark Academia

Use the attached photo as the subject. Keep the face, hair, body, pose and outfit exactly as they are, only the style changes.

STYLE: a dark academia aesthetic photo, a moody scholar in an old university world.

COLOUR: deep browns, forest green, burgundy and warm amber candle light. Desaturated and dark, no bright colours.

TEXTURE AND LIGHT: low warm light from a window or a lamp, deep shadows, soft film grain, a slightly hazy matte finish like an old photograph.

SCENE AND PROPS: tall wooden bookshelves full of leather books, gothic arched windows, rain outside, an open book, a cup of tea or coffee. The subject wears a wool coat, tweed blazer, knit vest or turtleneck.

COMPOSITION: quiet and contemplative, the subject reading, looking out of a window or walking between shelves, framed by architecture, lots of dark negative space.

AVOID: modern tech, bright daylight, logos, readable titles on the books, any text.

FORMAT: vertical 2:3, high resolution.

Cyberpunk

Use the attached photo as the subject. Keep the face, hair, body, pose and outfit exactly as they are, only the style changes.

STYLE: a cyberpunk city illustration, futuristic megacity at night like a sci fi film still.

COLOUR: neon magenta, electric cyan and violet against near black. Strong glowing highlights, deep blue shadows.

TEXTURE AND LIGHT: rain, wet streets with long neon reflections, light fog and haze between the buildings, glowing signs blooming into the air, slight digital painting finish.

SCENE: towering skyscrapers packed with holographic billboards and vertical neon signs, cables and walkways between buildings, flying cars far away. The subject stands small on the street or a rooftop, in techwear, seen against the light.

COMPOSITION: dramatic low angle looking up the canyon of buildings, strong one point perspective, the subject as a silhouette near the bottom third.

AVOID: daylight, readable brand names, real logos, clean empty streets.

FORMAT: vertical 2:3, high resolution.

Coquette

Use the attached photo as the subject. Keep the face, hair, body, pose and outfit exactly as they are, only the style changes.

STYLE: a coquette aesthetic photo, soft romantic and girly, like a dreamy diary snapshot.

COLOUR: blush pink, cream, ivory and soft red accents. Bright but gentle, creamy highlights, no harsh contrast.

TEXTURE AND LIGHT: window light or soft daylight, slight overexposure, light film grain, a hazy dreamy glow.

SCENE AND PROPS: satin ribbons and bows, lace, pearls, roses and peonies, cherries, a vintage mirror or candles. The subject in a white or pastel dress or a lace top, bows in the hair.

COMPOSITION: intimate and close, the subject lounging, holding flowers or taking a mirror selfie, props scattered around, cosy interior or a rose garden.

AVOID: dark moody tones, logos, magazine names, any text.

FORMAT: vertical 2:3, high resolution.

Grunge

Use the attached photo as the subject. Keep the face, hair, body, pose and outfit exactly as they are, only the style changes.

STYLE: a 90s grunge photo, raw and messy, shot on a cheap film camera.

COLOUR: faded olive, brown, washed out black and dirty red. Muted and slightly yellowed like an old print.

TEXTURE AND LIGHT: harsh on camera flash or dim room light, heavy grain, dust and scratches, slight blur, imperfect framing.

SCENE AND PROPS: a messy bedroom or a garage, posters on the wall, an electric guitar, vinyl records, worn sneakers. The subject in a flannel shirt, band style t shirt, ripped jeans and boots.

COMPOSITION: candid and careless, the subject sitting on the floor or bed, slouched, looking away, tight crop.

AVOID: clean studio look, readable band names, logos, any offensive words, any text.

FORMAT: vertical 2:3, high resolution.

Kawaii

Use the attached photo as the subject. Keep the face, hair, body, pose and outfit exactly as they are, only the style changes.

STYLE: a kawaii pastel illustration, super cute anime style artwork.

COLOUR: candy pink, lilac, baby blue, mint and cream. Soft pastel palette with white highlights and sparkles.

TEXTURE: clean digital illustration with soft shading, glossy eyes, tiny stars, hearts and sparkles floating around, sticker like outlines.

CHARACTER AND PROPS: the subject drawn as a cute anime character with big shiny eyes and blushing cheeks, same outfit in pastel colours, surrounded by tiny food, plush animals, strawberries, bows and clouds.

COMPOSITION: the character centred, full body or half body, cluttered cute background full of small decorations, cheerful pose.

AVOID: realistic photo, dark colours, existing characters, logos, any text.

FORMAT: vertical 2:3, high resolution.

Gothic

Use the attached photo as the subject. Keep the face, hair, body, pose and outfit exactly as they are, only the style changes.

STYLE: a gothic aesthetic photo, dark, romantic and eerie, like a black and white film still.

COLOUR: almost monochrome, cold greys and black with a slight blue or green tint. Optional single accent of deep red.

TEXTURE AND LIGHT: thick fog, overcast sky, soft diffused light, deep shadows, heavy film grain and a slight vignette.

SCENE AND PROPS: a misty cemetery with stone angels, gothic cathedral spires, pointed arched windows, bare trees, crows. The subject in long black clothes, velvet, lace, a wide brim hat.

COMPOSITION: the subject small and still in the middle of the fog or framed by an arch, lots of empty misty space, symmetrical and solemn.

AVOID: bright colours, sunshine, horror gore, logos, any text.

FORMAT: vertical 2:3, high resolution.
