Gold Foil

Use the attached photo as the subject. Keep the face, hair, body, pose and outfit exactly as they are, only the style changes.

STYLE: a luxury hot foil stamped print: the subject pressed in shiny metallic gold foil on premium paper, photographed as a real printed piece.

COLOUR: polished gold foil on [deep matte black] paper (or lavender grey, or warm ivory cotton paper). Only gold and the paper colour.

LINE: the subject drawn with fine elegant gold lines: contour lines and flowing parallel wave lines that follow the shape of the face, hair and clothes, like an engraving, with a few solid gold areas for the darkest parts. Optional delicate gold botanical line drawings (leaves, petals, ginkgo, branches) around the figure.

TEXTURE AND LIGHT: the foil slightly debossed into the soft paper, crisp edges, mirror reflections that shift from bright yellow to deep amber, fine paper fibre visible. Soft directional light from one side to make the foil glint.

TYPE: optional small foil text in a refined serif or thin sans in one corner: [YOUR NAME] and a short line like [Edition 01].

COMPOSITION: the printed card or poster lying at a slight angle on a dark surface, the subject centred on it with generous empty paper around.

AVOID: flat yellow ink instead of metal, glitter, cheap glossy plastic look, brand logos, more text.

FORMAT: vertical 2:3, high resolution.

Wood Carving

Use the attached photo as the subject. Keep the face, hair, body, pose and outfit exactly as they are, only the style changes.

STYLE: a hand carved wooden relief panel, the subject carved in high relief into a solid wood board.

COLOUR: warm natural wood: honey oak, walnut brown and light lime wood, darker in the deep cuts and lighter on the raised edges, no paint.

TEXTURE AND LIGHT: visible chisel and gouge marks, crisp undercuts, smooth sanded rounded volumes on the face, wood grain running vertically through the whole piece, small tool facets in the background. Warm side light from one corner so the relief casts deep shadows and every carved edge catches light.

SUBJECT: the person carved from the chest up or full body, hair as flowing carved strands, clothes as carved folds, face detailed and recognisable.

GRAPHICS: an ornamental carved frame around the figure: acanthus leaf scrolls, carved flowers, or oak leaves and acorns, inside a raised rectangular border.

SETTING: the panel lying on a wooden workbench with curls of wood shavings, a couple of carving gouges and a mallet at the edges.

COMPOSITION: panel seen almost straight on, filling most of the frame, a little workbench visible around it.

AVOID: painted wood, plastic look, laser cut flatness, signatures, any text.

FORMAT: vertical 2:3, high resolution.

Ceramic

Use the attached photo as the subject. Keep the face, hair, body, pose and outfit exactly as they are, only the style changes.

STYLE: a contemporary ceramic sculpture of the subject, photographed like a piece in a gallery or a design magazine.

MATERIAL: hand built stoneware with a matte speckled surface in [bone white] (or chalky grey, celadon blue grey, or rusty terracotta). Organic, flowing, sculptural forms: the body and hair turning into soft waves, curls and smooth hollow openings. Optional areas of glossy glaze with gentle drips running down, and a few painted porcelain details on the face.

TEXTURE AND LIGHT: fine clay grain and speckles, subtle tool marks and fingerprints, slightly uneven rims. One soft window light from the side, gentle gradients and a soft shadow on the floor.

SETTING: on a simple wooden block or concrete plinth against a plain seamless warm grey studio background.

SUBJECT: the person as a sculpted bust or full figure, face smooth and calm but clearly recognisable, proportions slightly elongated and stylised.

COMPOSITION: the sculpture centred with lots of empty space around, shot at eye level, minimal and quiet.

AVOID: shiny plastic, a painted figurine look, cluttered background, cracks or broken pieces, brand logos, any text.

FORMAT: vertical 2:3, high resolution.

Ice Sculpture

Use the attached photo as the subject. Keep the face, hair, body, pose and outfit exactly as they are, only the style changes.

STYLE: a real carved ice sculpture of the subject, photographed outdoors at a winter ice festival.

MATERIAL: crystal clear ice with refractions and internal reflections, frosted white carved lines for detail (hair strands, eyebrows, clothing folds), tiny trapped air bubbles, chisel and saw marks, sharp edges that melt slightly at the tips.

COLOUR: cold blue and cyan transparency, white frost, with the background seen through the ice and distorted. Warm golden and pink dusk sky (or soft cool blue light from inside the base).

LIGHT: backlight from a low sun or a light hidden in the plinth, making the ice glow and the edges sparkle, faint cold mist around the base.

SETTING: [a snowy park with tall dark pine trees at dusk] (or a frozen lake shore, or a plaza with ice arches), snow on the ground.

SUBJECT: the person carved as a full statue or a bust rising from a rough ice block plinth, face recognisable, with flowing ice ribbons and swirls around the figure.

COMPOSITION: statue centred, shot slightly from below, sky and trees behind.

AVOID: plastic or glass look, cartoon style, skin colour showing through, brand logos, any text.

FORMAT: vertical 2:3, high resolution.

Sand Art

Use the attached photo as the subject. Keep the face, hair, body, pose and outfit exactly as they are, only the style changes.

STYLE: a huge carved sand sculpture of the subject on a real beach, like a winning piece at a sand sculpture competition.

MATERIAL: fine compacted beach sand in warm beige and pale gold, crisp carved details, smooth patted surfaces, sharp cuts that show the grain, a few loose crumbs and small cracks.

LIGHT: low warm sun from one side, strong shadows inside the carved details, soft blue sky.

SETTING: wide sandy beach with the sea and a calm horizon behind, footprints in the sand around the base.

SUBJECT: the person sculpted as a bust or full figure, face detailed and recognisable, hair and clothes turning into flowing waves of sand that merge into the dune at the base.

COMPOSITION: sculpture centred and filling most of the frame, shot from a low angle so it feels monumental, sky at the top.

AVOID: plastic or stone look, wet mud colour, cartoon style, flags or brand logos, any text written in the sand.

FORMAT: vertical 2:3, high resolution.

Chocolate

Use the attached photo as the subject. Keep the face, hair, body, pose and outfit exactly as they are, only the style changes.

STYLE: a professional chocolate showpiece of the subject, like a pastry chef's competition sculpture.

MATERIAL: tempered dark, milk and white chocolate. Glossy smooth surfaces on the body, matte cocoa dusted areas, thin curved chocolate petals, ribbons and feathers, a few chocolate spheres sprayed with a velvet or metallic finish. Optional geometric faceted surfaces in some areas.

COLOUR: deep dark brown, warm milk brown and ivory white chocolate, with small accents of sugar work in [yellow and red] (flowers, berries).

TEXTURE AND LIGHT: soft studio light with rich highlights on the glossy chocolate, delicate edges, clean piping lines, tiny drops of chocolate.

SUBJECT: the person sculpted as a bust or full figure in chocolate, face recognisable, hair as flowing ribbons of chocolate, clothes as layered thin chocolate sheets.

SETTING: on a round chocolate base on a [white marble plate], against a plain dark grey backdrop (or a competition table with a white cloth).

COMPOSITION: centred, shot at eye level, sculpture tall in the vertical frame with space around.

AVOID: melted mess, a cake, cartoon look, plastic shine, brand logos, any text.

FORMAT: vertical 2:3, high resolution.

Paper Mache

Use the attached photo as the subject. Keep the face, hair, body, pose and outfit exactly as they are, only the style changes.

STYLE: a handmade paper mache figure of the subject, photographed at home like a craft piece.

MATERIAL: layered strips of glued newspaper, visible edges and tiny bits of printed text under the paint, lumpy uneven surfaces, slight wrinkles and bumps, wire whiskers or details where needed.

COLOUR: painted with bright acrylics: [cobalt blue, lime green, orange, hot pink and yellow], decorated with dots, stripes, circles and folk style patterns, glossy varnish on some parts. (Alternative: left mostly raw with natural paper colour and a little white paint.)

TEXTURE AND LIGHT: brush strokes, small drips and smudges, soft daylight from a window, gentle shadows.

SUBJECT: the person as a slightly exaggerated figurine or bust, big expressive eyes, long neck, recognisable hairstyle and outfit colours, charming and a bit clumsy.

SETTING: on a wooden shelf or table near a window, a vase of dried flowers and a few books blurred behind.

COMPOSITION: figure centred, shot at eye level with a shallow depth of field.

AVOID: smooth plastic, ceramic look, clean factory finish, readable newspaper headlines, brand logos, other text.

FORMAT: vertical 2:3, high resolution.
