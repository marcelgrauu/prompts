INSTRUCTIONS FOR THE AGENT
Generate ONE brand new video from scratch with Seedance 2.5. Ignore any previous version, this is a new generation.
Do not switch to any other model, even if another one seems better or faster.
Settings: vertical 9:16, 10 seconds, highest quality available.
Reference: use the attached image (called Image 1 below) ONLY as the reference for the product, the set and the style. It is NOT the first frame and NOT the last frame, and no shot copies its exact framing.
Use the video prompt below exactly as written. Do not rewrite it, shorten it, translate it or split it into separate clips. It is one continuous edit with 11 shots and hard cuts, generated in one go.
Audio: dark, elegant luxury fashion music, minimal and confident, deep pulsing bass, crisp percussion hits that land on every cut, a short suspense build before the bottle drops and a satisfying hit when the king falls. Subtle sound effects on top: soft clicks of pieces, the pawn knocked off the board, the thud of the bottle landing, the king falling and rolling, a whoosh on the orbit. No voice, no narration.
Do not add text, captions, logos or watermarks to the video.
Do not ask questions, generate directly.

VIDEO PROMPT
Luxury product commercial, 10 seconds, vertical 9:16, fast and dynamic, 11 short shots.

PRODUCT RULE (most important): the product is the blue frosted glass OLTA bottle from Image 1: short rounded bottle in deep blue frosted glass, a LIGHT PASTEL POWDER BLUE cap, and a white label reading OLTA and TONIC. Keep its shape, size, colours and label EXACTLY as in Image 1 in every shot. The cap is light pastel powder blue in EVERY single shot, wide shots, close ups and macros included. The cap is NEVER pink, red, magenta or purple, even when the burgundy background reflects on it. Never add, change or invent text or logos on the label.

LOOK
Set: a lacquered black and white chessboard with a mirror gloss finish, on a seamless deep burgundy background.
Props: classic black and white chess pieces. A woman's hand in a black satin opera glove wearing a diamond tennis bracelet and two large diamond cocktail rings. Only this one hand ever appears, always with five fingers and the same glove and jewellery.
Camera: mix of macro probe lens, overhead top down, board level low angles and 65mm cinematic. Every shot from a different angle. Ultra sharp textures.
Light: hard frontal flash key with a soft rim light, crisp shadows, bright specular highlights on the frosted glass, the lacquer and the diamonds. Consistent light and colour in all shots, no flicker.
Grade: rich burgundy, deep clean blacks, bright whites, the blue bottle and its pastel blue cap as the only cool colours, high contrast, editorial luxury, very subtle film grain.
No faces, no on screen text, no other brands. Fast rhythm, every cut lands on the beat, motion precise and elegant, realistic physics and weight, no morphing.

00:00 to 00:01.6 · Shot 1: The capture (overhead)
Top down view of the board in the middle of a game, pieces scattered. A black pawn stands on a square. The gloved hand picks up a white knight, jumps it in a proper L shape and sets it down ON the black pawn's square: at the exact moment of contact the knight knocks the pawn away, the pawn tumbles off the board and out of frame, and the knight stays on that square. The knight never lands next to the pawn first. One continuous fluid move. CUT

00:01.6 to 00:02.4 · Shot 2: Diamonds in passing (candid macro)
The hand keeps playing: it sweeps across the board to grab another piece. The macro camera is already placed low near the pieces and the hand simply passes through the frame on its way, it does not stop and it does not turn toward the lens. As the rings cross a beam of light, one diamond flares for a split second, with natural motion blur and the focus catching it just in time, like a lucky documentary moment. The hand is in frame for less than a second and exits while still moving. Not a posed product shot of the ring. CUT

00:02.4 to 00:03.1 · Shot 3: Tension (low angle)
The black king stands alone in the centre of the board, towering in a low hero angle, its mirror reflection below. A beat of stillness, the music holds. CUT

00:03.1 to 00:04.0 · Shot 4: The drop
Wide: out of nowhere the OLTA bottle with its pastel blue cap drops from above into frame and lands upright on the board with a heavy confident thud, a ripple of light across the lacquer. CUT

00:04.0 to 00:04.7 · Shot 5: Impact (macro)
Macro at board level: the base of the bottle touching the lacquer, its reflection snapping into place. CUT

00:04.7 to 00:05.6 · Shot 6: Checkmate
Side angle: the bottle slides one square forward and knocks over the black king, which topples in slow motion. Pastel blue cap clearly visible. CUT

00:05.6 to 00:06.3 · Shot 7: The king rolls (macro)
Extreme macro: the fallen black king rolls on the glossy board and stops, its crown filling the frame. CUT

00:06.3 to 00:07.0 · Shot 8: Glass (macro)
Macro on the frosted blue glass, tiny cold condensation beads, a streak of light sliding across the surface. CUT

00:07.0 to 00:07.6 · Shot 9: Cap and label (macro)
Macro tilt from the light pastel powder blue cap down to the label, OLTA and TONIC sharp and identical to Image 1. CUT

00:07.6 to 00:08.3 · Shot 10: Orbit
Quick half orbit around the bottle at board level, the white knight and pawn passing in the foreground, the fallen king behind. Pastel blue cap. CUT

00:08.3 to 00:10 · Shot 11: Final wide (in motion)
Start close: the gloved fingertips lift off the pastel blue cap and the hand withdraws up and out of frame. The camera keeps moving the whole time: a smooth continuous pull back and rise to a wide, slightly high angle of the whole set: the OLTA bottle alone in the centre of the board as the winner, the fallen black king at its feet, the white knight, the white pawn and a few other pieces around, mirror reflections below, deep burgundy background. A slow streak of light slides across the glass. The camera is still gently drifting on the very last frame. It must not look like a still photo and must not copy the framing of Image 1. No fade.
