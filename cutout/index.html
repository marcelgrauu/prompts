How to use it

Photo, prompt, send.

  1. Open ChatGPT and attach one photo of yourself.
  2. Copy the prompt and paste it in the same message.
  3. Send it. If you want another version, just send it again.
Turn this photo into a minimalist editorial collage for a luxury travel magazine. Warm ivory paper background with lots of empty space. Place a large vertical rectangle in the centre, slightly above the middle, with a clean crop of my photo inside. Let me and the ground at the bottom break out of the bottom edge of the frame and spill into the ivory space with a soft natural cutout edge. Keep my face, outfit and pose exactly the same.

Tips

Get a better one.

  1. Full body photos work best: the feet and the ground are what break out of the frame.
  2. Pick a photo with something on the ground: rocks, grass, sand, water.
  3. If the cutout looks too clean, ask: make the bottom edge more organic.
