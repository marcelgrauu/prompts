Liquify

Use the attached photo as the subject. Keep the face, hair, body, pose and outfit exactly as they are, only the style changes.

STYLE: a surreal liquify portrait, as if parts of the photo were pushed and smeared with a digital smudge tool until they turned into liquid.

EFFECT: the face stays sharp and recognisable around the eyes and mouth, while chosen areas melt: hair stretches sideways in long smooth ribbons, one side of the face and shoulder drags out of the frame in parallel streaks, skin flows into glossy swirls like wet paint or molten metal. Optional: a horizontal band across the eyes slides sideways and stretches into thin lines.

COLOUR: keep the photo's natural colours, or choose a variant: [IRIDESCENT CHROME] (rose gold and silver with black reflections) or [ACID] (mint green and cream on black).

TEXTURE AND LIGHT: smooth continuous distortion with no pixel blocks, glossy highlights following the curves of the warped areas like poured liquid, soft studio light, a dark or plain grey background so the flowing shapes read clearly.

COMPOSITION: head and shoulders portrait, the melt flowing in one direction towards one edge of the frame, with empty space for it to travel into.

AVOID: random glitch blocks, pixel sorting, melting the whole face away, horror, brand logos, any text.

FORMAT: vertical 2:3, high resolution.

Shattered Glass

Use the attached photo as the subject. Keep the face, hair, body, pose and outfit exactly as they are, only the style changes.

STYLE: a portrait seen through shattered glass or a broken mirror, photographed for real.

GLASS: one impact point near the face or chest sends sharp radial cracks out to the edges, crossed by rings of smaller cracks. The image is split into sharp shards, each slightly displaced, tilted or offset, so parts of the face do not line up perfectly. A few small fragments and splinters fly off and catch the light.

LIGHT: hard light that makes the crack lines glint white and silver, a bright starburst of refraction at the impact point, reflections along the shard edges.

COLOUR: [DRAMATIC BLACK AND WHITE] with deep blacks, or a sunny analog colour photo with saturated reds and a clear blue sky.

TEXTURE: film grain, razor sharp glass edges, a little dust on the glass, some shards with grey backing showing through like pieces of a mirror lying on the ground.

COMPOSITION: close up of the face partly crossed by cracks with one eye clear and sharp, or a half body shot behind a cracked pane. The shatter is the dominant shape of the image.

AVOID: blood, cuts, violence, a flat crack overlay texture, a cartoon look, brand logos, any text.

FORMAT: vertical 2:3, high resolution.

Smoke

Use the attached photo as the subject. Keep the face, hair, body, pose and outfit exactly as they are, only the style changes.

STYLE: a fine art smoke portrait, a black and white studio photo where the subject turns into smoke.

COLOUR: pure black background, smoke in white and silver grey (or a warm cream sepia variant). Skin in deep monochrome.

EFFECT: the face is sharp and real from the eyes down (nose, lips and chin), while the top of the head and the hair dissolve upward into curling plumes of smoke. The smoke rises in elegant swirling ribbons, wisps and soft curls, getting thinner and more transparent as it climbs. A thin stream of smoke rises from the lips.

LIGHT: one low hard light from below or the side, deep shadows that melt into the black background, smoke glowing where the light hits it.

TEXTURE: fine silky detail inside the smoke, high detail skin with a soft sheen, slight grain.

COMPOSITION: vertical and symmetrical, face in the lower third, smoke filling the top two thirds of the frame.

AVOID: cigarettes, fire, colour, a grey background, fog that hides the face completely, brand logos, any text.

FORMAT: vertical 2:3, high resolution.

Fire

Use the attached photo as the subject. Keep the face, hair, body, pose and outfit exactly as they are, only the style changes.

STYLE: a fire art portrait: real looking flames around and on the subject, shot like a cinematic analog photo.

EFFECT: choose one: flames rising from the head and shoulders like a burning crown; the whole figure wrapped in one tall bright flame like a torch; fire textures projected over the face and chest like glowing patterns on the skin; or the subject standing in front of a huge bonfire as a dark silhouette. The face always stays recognisable.

COLOUR: white and yellow in the hot core, orange and deep red at the edges, against a dark background or a flat saturated backdrop: [DEEP RED] or [PEACH]. Skin warm from the firelight.

LIGHT: the fire is the main light source, warm glow on the skin, flickering highlights, embers and sparks floating in the air.

TEXTURE: analog film grain, slight motion blur on the flame tips, heat haze rippling near the flames.

COMPOSITION: subject centred, half body or three quarter shot, fire filling the upper part of the frame, dark space around.

AVOID: injuries, smoke covering the face, cartoon flames, a flat fire filter, brand logos, any text.

FORMAT: vertical 2:3, high resolution.

Fractal

Use the attached photo as the subject. Keep the face, hair, body, pose and outfit exactly as they are, only the style changes.

STYLE: a fractal art portrait, the subject embedded in an infinite fractal pattern rendered as high detail digital art.

PATTERN: the subject sits in the middle of a deep fractal spiral that keeps repeating smaller copies of itself: logarithmic spirals, curling seahorse tails, feathery fern branches, rims of tiny bead like spheres. The pattern wraps around the figure and flows into the hair and clothes, the edges of the body breaking into smaller and smaller swirls.

COLOUR: vivid jewel tones: turquoise, magenta, violet, gold and orange, with glossy metallic gradients and a deep black or indigo void at the centre of the spiral. Variant: [GOLD AND SILVER] for a baroque look.

TEXTURE AND LIGHT: glossy, almost metallic sheen on every curl, fine thread like lines like peacock feathers, inner glow, razor sharp detail at every scale.

COMPOSITION: face sharp and fully recognisable in the centre, the spiral rotating around the subject, the pattern filling the frame edge to edge.

AVOID: hiding the face, blurry or low detail areas, a simple mirrored kaleidoscope, brand logos, any text.

FORMAT: vertical 2:3, high resolution.

Ink in Water

Use the attached photo as the subject. Keep the face, hair, body, pose and outfit exactly as they are, only the style changes.

STYLE: an ink in water photograph: clouds of ink poured into a clear water tank and frozen with high speed photography, forming the figure of the subject.

EFFECT: the head and shoulders built from dense billowing ink: thick rolling cauliflower like clouds, curling plumes and fine threads trailing down. The face is readable in the folds of ink, with light and shadow modelling the features. A stream of ink falls from the top of the frame into the head, as if it was just poured in.

COLOUR: one or two inks: [MAGENTA AND TEAL] (or deep black, coral pink, or cobalt blue with purple). Saturated where the ink is dense, transparent where it thins out.

BACKGROUND: clean bright white or pale peach water, seamless, no visible tank edges.

TEXTURE AND LIGHT: soft even studio light from behind, velvety smooth ink surfaces, fine wisps and tiny droplets, sharp focus.

COMPOSITION: one vertical plume with the figure in the middle, the ink trail coming from the top and spreading out at the bottom.

AVOID: smoke, paint splashes, hard outlines, a photo with an ink overlay, brand logos, any text.

FORMAT: vertical 2:3, high resolution.

Moire

Use the attached photo as the subject. Keep the face, hair, body, pose and outfit exactly as they are, only the style changes.

STYLE: a moire pattern image: the subject built from overlapping fine line patterns that create shimmering interference waves, like op art and Swiss design posters.

EFFECT: two layers of thin parallel lines, one straight and one bending with the shape of the face and body, overlap and interfere. Where they meet they make flowing ripples, contour rings and wavy bands that model the volumes of the face, hair and clothes like a topographic map. Line thickness and spacing change to create light and shadow.

COLOUR: [BLACK AND WHITE] by default, or a two colour version: red and cobalt blue, pale teal on black, or magenta on bright orange.

TEXTURE: crisp vector lines, a slight optical vibration, no gradients, no blur, a subtle printed paper feel.

TYPE (optional): small Swiss style info text at the bottom in a clean grotesque sans, aligned to a grid: [FRIDAY 14 JANUARY], [THE BASEMENT], [LIVE ELECTRONICS].

COMPOSITION: face and shoulders filling most of the frame, background made of straight lines so the interference is visible around the outline.

AVOID: a photo with lines on top, solid colour areas, blurry gradients, brand logos, real names.

FORMAT: vertical 2:3, high resolution.
