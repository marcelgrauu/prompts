Thermal

Use the attached photo as the subject. Keep the face, hair, body, pose and outfit exactly as they are, only the style changes.

STYLE: a real thermal imaging camera capture (FLIR style) turned into a graphic poster.

COLOUR: false colour "ironbow" heat palette. The warmest skin (face, neck, hands) glows white and pale yellow, the body is hot orange and red, clothes cool down into magenta and purple, and the background is cold deep blue, indigo and violet. No natural colours left anywhere in the image.

TEXTURE AND LIGHT: soft heat gradients instead of shadows, a glowing rim where the body meets the air, clothing folds readable only as temperature changes, slight sensor noise and a little softness like a low resolution sensor.

INTERFACE: thin white monospace HUD on top. A vertical temperature scale on the right edge, from blue at the bottom to white at the top, labelled 20.0 to 40.0 °C. A small square target box on the chest with 36.5°C next to it. Top left a red dot and REC 00:00:12. Bottom left tiny lines: Emissivity 0.95, Distance 1.0 m. Thin corner brackets framing the shot.

COMPOSITION: full body or three quarter shot, subject centred, plenty of cold dark space around so the heat pops.

AVOID: normal photo colours, cartoon or painted look, brand logos, any text beyond the HUD.

FORMAT: vertical 2:3, high resolution.

ASCII Art

STYLE: an ASCII art portrait, the subject rebuilt entirely from monospace text characters.

CHARACTERS: only letters, numbers and symbols set in a fixed grid of rows: dense heavy characters like @ # M W 8 $ for the darkest areas, medium ones like o x = + for mid tones, light ones like . , : ' for highlights, and empty spaces for pure white. Tone comes only from character density, no lines or shading.

COLOUR: choose one: [black characters on white paper like a printer output], [light grey characters on black like a terminal], or [green characters on black].

TEXTURE: crisp characters readable up close, while from a distance the face appears clearly and recognisably. Slight printer unevenness or a soft screen glow.

TYPE: a small monospace caption under the portrait: [Portrait of YOUR NAME] on the left and a date like 29/10/2026 on the right.

COMPOSITION: head and shoulders portrait centred, background left empty, the edges of the figure dissolving into sparser characters.

AVOID: solid shapes, pixel blocks, smooth gradients, a photo visible underneath, brand logos.

FORMAT: vertical 2:3, high resolution.

CCTV

Use the attached photo as the subject. Keep the face, hair, body, pose and outfit exactly as they are, only the style changes.

STYLE: a still frame from a security camera, the kind of grainy found footage clip that gets shared online.

ANGLE: a camera mounted high in a corner near the ceiling, looking steeply down at the subject, wide lens with slight barrel distortion so walls, shelves and doors lean.

SETTING: an everyday place: [a corner shop aisle with full shelves and drink fridges] (or a building entrance with glass doors, an empty street at night, a supermarket corridor). Products and signs blurred into anonymous colour blocks.

COLOUR: washed out low quality video colours with a cold green or blue cast under fluorescent light, or black and white infrared at night. Flat contrast, blown out light sources.

TEXTURE: low resolution, heavy compression blocks, smeared motion on hands and feet, noise in the shadows, slight interlacing lines.

INTERFACE: white monospace overlay: top left the date and a running time like 23.02 FRI 22:05:43, top right CAM 03, bottom left a red dot and REC. A thin green rectangle detection box around the subject with a small label person 0.89. Optionally a zoomed inset box of the face in a corner, linked to the subject with thin lines.

COMPOSITION: full body, subject in the lower middle of the frame with lots of floor around, caught mid action, either looking up at the camera or unaware of it.

AVOID: cinematic lighting, sharp detail, an eye level angle, readable product names, brand logos.
