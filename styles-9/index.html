Prism

Use the attached photo as the subject. Keep the face, hair, body, pose and outfit exactly as they are, only the style changes.

STYLE: an editorial portrait photographed through a glass prism held in front of the lens, with rainbow light falling across the subject.

COLOUR: natural skin and the real colours of the photo, crossed by vivid spectral bands: red, orange, yellow, green, cyan, blue and violet. Warm golden backlight elsewhere.

LIGHT: hard sunlight split by the prism into sharp rainbow stripes that fall across the face (over one eye, a cheek or the lips), plus soft rainbow flares and streaks floating in front of the lens. Optional warm backlight glowing around the head.

TEXTURE: a soft ghost double image where the prism reflects part of the subject, a translucent copy of the face shifted to one side, blurred glass edges in the foreground, subtle film grain, very shallow depth of field.

TYPE: optional, one minimal word in widely spaced white sans serif capitals over the image: [YOUR WORD].

COMPOSITION: tight close up of the face (eyes, nose, lips) or a three quarter portrait, slightly off centre, the rainbow band crossing diagonally.

AVOID: a fake digital rainbow overlay, a fully oversaturated image, glitch effects, brand logos, any other text.

FORMAT: vertical 2:3, high resolution.

Kaleidoscope

Use the attached photo as the subject. Keep the face, hair, body, pose and outfit exactly as they are, only the style changes.

STYLE: the view through a real kaleidoscope: the photo mirrored into a perfect symmetrical mandala of 6 to 12 segments around the centre.

COLOUR: the colours of the photo intensified into jewel brights: electric blue, violet, magenta, amber and emerald with sparkling white, or a softer silver and grey mirror version.

TEXTURE AND LIGHT: crisp mirror seams radiating from the centre, faceted crystal shards, rainbow fringes on the edges, glints, star shaped sparkles and lens flares where light hits the glass, a sense of depth like looking down a mirrored tunnel.

SUBJECT: the subject's face clearly visible in the centre, with its reflections repeated in rings around it, getting smaller towards the core or the edges, forming a flower of faces and eyes.

COMPOSITION: radial symmetry centred in the frame, the pattern filling the whole image to the edges, a slight dark vignette.

AVOID: a random collage, blurry mirrors, a flat vector mandala, brand logos, any text.

FORMAT: vertical 2:3, high resolution.

Slit Scan

Use the attached photo as the subject. Keep the face, hair, body, pose and outfit exactly as they are, only the style changes.

STYLE: a slit scan photograph, recorded one line at a time while the subject moved, so time stretches parts of the body into long smooth ribbons.

EFFECT: parts of the subject (an arm, the hair, the side of the head, the torso) smear and stretch into long continuous bands that flow sideways or spiral around the body like elastic ribbons of colour. The eyes and most of the face stay sharp and recognisable. The background (a wall, a whiteboard, a park path) stays still and sharp, so the stretching reads as time.

COLOUR: the real natural colours of the photo. Each streak keeps the exact colour of the part it comes from (skin, a striped shirt, a blue sweater) and gets more saturated where it stretches.

TEXTURE: clean photo, fine parallel streak lines along the stretched bands, slight banding where the scan repeats, no motion blur.

COMPOSITION: subject centred or slightly off centre, half or full body, the ribbons sweeping across the frame in one direction (horizontal, or a spiral around the body), plain background around.

AVOID: motion blur, ghostly double exposure, glitch blocks, melted 3D, watermarks, brand logos, any text.

FORMAT: vertical 2:3, high resolution.

Light Painting

Use the attached photo as the subject. Keep the face, hair, body, pose and outfit exactly as they are, only the style changes.

STYLE: a long exposure light painting photo at night: the subject stands still in darkness while glowing ribbons of coloured light are drawn around them.

COLOUR: deep black background, light trails in neon colours: electric blue, cyan, hot pink, magenta, red and warm orange, some with white hot cores. Skin lit only by the trails, taking their colours.

LIGHT AND TEXTURE: smooth continuous ribbons of light of varying width, twisting, looping and spiralling around the body, some passing in front and some behind. Bright cores fading into soft glowing edges, a little bloom, reflections on a wet floor. Optional: a big ring of burning sparks behind the subject, or streams of light flowing down from the eyes like tears. Subject sharp, trails crisp.

SETTING: a dark empty space: a black studio, a concrete underpass, a tunnel with faint graffiti, or a pier at dusk with a deep blue sky.

COMPOSITION: subject centred, full body, three quarter or a close portrait, light trails wrapping and framing them, lots of black space.

AVOID: daylight, a blurry subject, a fake digital line overlay, fireworks, watermarks, brand logos, any text.

FORMAT: vertical 2:3, high resolution.

Bokeh

Use the attached photo as the subject. Keep the face, hair, body, pose and outfit exactly as they are, only the style changes.

STYLE: a cinematic night portrait with dreamy bokeh, shot with an 85 mm portrait lens wide open at f1.2.

COLOUR: warm golden amber and soft white points of light against deep shadows, touches of green from foliage or a cool blue night sky. Skin warm and natural.

LIGHT: the subject lit softly by the surrounding fairy lights and city lights (warm glow on the face, a gentle rim light on the hair). Background and foreground full of big round soft bokeh circles of different sizes. Optional: a string of fairy lights close to the lens so blurred orbs float in front of the face, or a sparkler in one hand throwing sparks.

TEXTURE: very shallow depth of field, eyes razor sharp, creamy soft falloff, light film grain, a faint ring texture inside the bokeh circles.

COMPOSITION: head and shoulders or half body, subject slightly off centre, points of light filling the edges of the frame, dark moody background.

AVOID: harsh flash, hexagonal digital bokeh, cartoon glow, logos on clothes, brand logos, any text.

FORMAT: vertical 2:3, high resolution.
06 / 07

Film Burn

Use the attached photo as the subject. Keep the face, hair, body, pose and outfit exactly as they are, only the style changes.

STYLE: an analog film photo damaged by heat and light: the emulsion melting and burning at the edges, like a frame stuck in a hot projector.

COLOUR: faded warm film colours: teal shadows, amber and peach highlights. The burn areas glow hot orange, red, yellow and white, edged with black char. Some areas bleached milky white or purple from chemical damage.

TEXTURE: bubbling, blistering emulsion with organic holes spreading outwards, charred brown and black edges with glowing orange rims, chemical stains, red and orange light leaks bleeding across part of the frame, heavy grain, dust and scratches, slight softness from a cheap lens.

GRAPHICS: optional film strip borders with sprocket holes on one or both sides, the burn eating into them.

SUBJECT: the subject in a soft natural photo (by the sea at dusk, hair blowing in the wind, or a moody close up), clearly visible, with the burn spreading around them or a hole opening near them but never destroying the face.

COMPOSITION: subject in the centre or slightly off centre, the damage strongest at the edges and in one corner, as if the burning just started.

AVOID: clean digital fire, flames drawn on top, digital glitch, brand logos, any text or numbers on the film edge.

FORMAT: vertical 2:3, high resolution.

Lenticular

Use the attached photo as the subject. Keep the face, hair, body, pose and outfit exactly as they are, only the style changes.

STYLE: a lenticular or folded prism art piece: the image printed across fine vertical ridges, so it looks dimensional and shifts with the viewing angle.

EFFECT: the whole image cut into thin vertical strips like a folded accordion or ribbed plastic. Two views of the subject are interlaced: the main portrait plus a second slightly shifted version (another angle, or the same face in another colour treatment), creating a shimmering 3D double image. Each ridge has one lit side and one shaded side.

COLOUR: either a black and white portrait with deep greys, or bold flat colours (yellow, purple, teal, red) on alternating facets, with a faint rainbow sheen on the plastic ridges.

TEXTURE AND LIGHT: regular visible ridges across the entire image, highlights running down each ridge edge, slight shadows between the folds, a hint of glossy plastic.

SETTING: the piece shown as a framed panel on a gallery wall or as a large mural on a ribbed metal shutter, photographed slightly from the side so the folds read clearly.

COMPOSITION: face and shoulders large in the centre, ridges running vertically through the whole frame, including the face.

AVOID: horizontal lines, glitch effects, a flat image with stripes simply painted on, brand logos, any text.

FORMAT: vertical 2:3, high resolution.
